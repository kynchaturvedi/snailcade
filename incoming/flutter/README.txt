Flutter (PICO-8) - offline copy

flutter.p8.png  - PICO-8 cartridge. Load in PICO-8, or in an arcade/emulator frontend with a PICO-8 core (e.g. RetroArch fake-08 / retro8).
flutter.p8.rom  - same cartridge as a raw ROM (PICO-8 0.2+).
web/index.html  - standalone offline browser version. Open it in Chrome/Firefox, click or press a key to start.
                  Controls: arrow keys, Z/X (or a gamepad).
