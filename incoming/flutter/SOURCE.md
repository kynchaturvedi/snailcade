# Flutter (PICO-8), incoming source

Flutter is a PICO-8 game made by a friend of the Director. The Director wants it added to the arcade as a gift.

- Original page: https://arcade.valley.games/games/flutter?playMode=free
- `flutter.p8.png` is the real PICO-8 cartridge (cart name `flutter-valley.p8`); `flutter.p8.rom` is the ROM.
- `web/index.html` + `web/flutter.js` are the exported web player; tested, boots to the title screen.
- `cover.gif` and `flutter-title.png` are the cover art and a title-screen capture.

This folder is a drop-off. Move or wire it into the arcade however fits the shelf.
