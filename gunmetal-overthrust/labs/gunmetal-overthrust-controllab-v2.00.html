CONTROLS
Phone, left thumbMove. Push clearly UP to jump; keep holding to fly (burns EN). A diagonal run never jumps. Push clearly DOWN on a girder to drop through. Scheme B: a quick sideways FLICK is a boost dash.
Phone, right thumbRelative to the target, not the screen: push TOWARD it fires the machine gun (hold keeps firing); UP charges the cannon (release to fire; the phone buzzes when full); DOWN fires the missiles; flick AWAY cycles targets; hold AWAY drops the lock. Scheme A: a quick TAP is a boost dash.
GamepadLeft stick moves; A jumps and flies; B boosts; RT machine gun, LT charge cannon, RB missiles; LB cycles targets (hold LB to drop the lock).
KeyboardA D or arrows move; Space or W jumps and flies; S drops; Shift boosts; J K L fire; Tab cycles (hold Tab to drop the lock).
ForgivingA jump still counts just after leaving a ledge, and a jump pressed just before landing is remembered. The pit costs armor (AP), not a life.
NOTES (append only, below the controls)

What to judge. Your thumbs pick the scheme: A (boost on a right-stick tap, your first scheme) or B (boost on a left-stick flick, pitch section 5). Try both on the strip: the girders, the pit, the targets, and the SENTRY turret on the right that shoots back.

What to judge in v2.00. The feel you called stiff. The jump now squats before it leaves the ground, rises high while you hold up and cuts short when you let go, falls faster than it rises, and lands with a bounce and dust; reversing at speed skids; the walk strides with the ground. The guns kick: the machine gun flashes and throws casings, the cannon blasts with a ring and a shake, the missiles pop the shoulder pods. The mech is the art lab's new VANGUARD, on the same street.

The device line in the bar says what this page was allowed to do. VIBRATE reads "accepted" when the phone took a buzz, "refused" or "blocked" when the viewer stopped it (try BUZZ). GAMEPAD reads "connected" once a pad is seen (press a button on it), "blocked" when the viewer forbids it. Inside a claude.ai page either may be blocked; if so, say which, and the seat asks the Studio Head for a public test page.
