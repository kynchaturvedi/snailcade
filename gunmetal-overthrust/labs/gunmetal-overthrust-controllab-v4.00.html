NOTES (append only, below the controls)

What to judge. Your thumbs pick the scheme: A (boost on a right-stick tap, your first scheme) or B (boost on a left-stick flick, pitch section 5). Try both on the strip: the girders, the pit, the targets, and the SENTRY turret on the right that shoots back.

What to judge in v2.00. The feel you called stiff. The jump now crouches for three frames before it leaves the ground (a run keeps its speed through it); a quick tap of up hops about 36 px, over the low girder, and holding up jumps higher and then flies; it falls faster than it rises and lands with a bounce and dust; while a foot is on a ledge the mech still stands on it, or lands on it; reversing at speed skids; the walk strides with the ground. The guns kick: the machine gun flashes and throws casings, the cannon blasts with a ring and a shake, the missiles pop the shoulder pods. The mech is the art lab's new VANGUARD, on the same street. For the phone's buzz and a gamepad, open the public copy of this same file (its link is on the title page): a claude.ai page may block both.

What to judge in v3.00. Your second round of notes. The VANGUARD is redrawn bigger, chunkier and richer (50 px tall, up from 45, and far wider, near the SD references' texel count), and it moves on keyframes that play smoothly at any speed: the trot, the breathing idle, the eye that scans. WEAPON swaps the main weapon, and each fights its own way: the RC-9 rifle fires steadily (its bolt throws brass, its drum turns); the GR-4 gatling spins up before it fires, then fires fast and wide and its barrels glow; the HB-9 bazooka rides the shoulder and fires one heavy rocket with a back-blast, a splash, and a reload; the HX heat blade swings (push toward the target) with a smear and a lunge.

What to judge in v4.00. Your third round of notes. The VANGUARD is scaled up to 64 px (about 18 metres on this street) and redrawn part by part so head, torso and weapon each read on their own: the gold crest, the visor where the eye scans, the breathing chest lens, the pods pauldron, the spiral shield with twin boosters over it. Its arm now hangs from under the pauldron to the gun, and every weapon is redrawn bigger, with bigger flames, a bigger blast and a blade arc that sweeps from overhead to the strike. A hard shot rocks the torso back a pixel. The dark outline no longer sinks into the night: the sky's light edges the back and the top of the mech (open HELP and set LIGHT to NIGHT to see it). The hurt box and the ceiling grow with the mech; the blade reaches as far as it is drawn.
