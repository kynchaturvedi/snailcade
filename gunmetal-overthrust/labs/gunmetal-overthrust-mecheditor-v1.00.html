CONTROLS
KeyboardA D or arrows move; Space, W or up jumps, hold it to fly (on treads: hold it to lift off); S drops through a girder; Shift dashes; J K L fire. The lab's keys: T (or M) opens the editor, P pauses, . steps one frame, - and = slow motion down and up, R resets the strip, 1 to 4 pick the weapon, F full screen, Z scale, G grid, N light, H or ? shows every key. Inside a viewer the page hears keys only after a click on the game; while you type a name, the keys stay in the box.
PhoneLeft thumb moves (push clearly up to jump, keep holding to fly); right thumb fights, relative to the target. EDIT opens the editor; on a wide screen the game moves over and keeps playing beside it.
GamepadLeft stick moves; A jumps and flies; B dashes; RT fires.
Reading itTop left: the build's weight against its load and its armor, and on treads how far the lift-off has spooled. Top right, the move lab's readouts: the state and speeds, the last time in the air, frames to top speed and to a stop.
NOTES (append only, below the controls)

What this is. Pitch section 10, step 2, and section 9's first mech editor: create and tune parts, assemble a test mech, try it at once on the test strip, keep the work in the browser, export a file. The mech you assemble is the one you drive: every change lands the next frame.

What to judge. Whether the parts pull a build the way the pitch says (section 4): the legs choose the rules and carry the load, everything weighs, and weight past the load slows the whole mech; the head sets the lock range, the core and the generator the energy, the booster the thrust and the dash, the arms the recoil. Put the HEAVY parts on the KESTREL legs and feel the overload; put them on the MULE treads and they carry it. THE BUILD measures each change on the LEG LAB's runs.

Making parts. TUNE A PART changes the numbers of the part in the chosen slot. NEW VARIANT copies it under a new name with its own numbers and the same pixels (a dashed button in ASSEMBLE); new pixels come from the seat for now. BUILDS keeps up to twelve named builds. FILE: EXPORT or COPY puts the whole work in the box as JSON, SAVE FILE saves it where the page may save files (the public test site; a viewer may block it, then use COPY), and IMPORT reads a pasted file back. Paste an export to the seat and it goes into the game as it is.

Provenance. The parts, their pixels and their numbers, the street and the mover are the game's own: go-art.js, go-parts.js, go-mech.js and go-view.js v6.00, go-shell.js v1.02, go-play.js v1.01 (the play core the other play labs share), go-tune.js v1.01 (the move lab's readouts and base panel). Assembled by build-labs.js from verbatim slices, each named with its sha256 in the page source.
